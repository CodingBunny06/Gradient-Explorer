# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Gradient Explorer aims to be a respectful, constructive space for learning and improving an educational software project. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews, or other project spaces is expected to help make participation welcoming and professional.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback.
- Provide clear, relevant technical context.
- Respect differing levels of experience and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insults, intimidation, or deliberately hostile communication.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or other disruptive conduct.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports of unacceptable behavior may be sent privately to the maintainer through GitHub. The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation where necessary.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by common open-source community standards, including the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
